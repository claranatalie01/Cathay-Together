Flow review: what is still missing

Preview only. Nothing in the app has been changed. Phones are 440 px wide, same tokens and icon files as the app. Orange tags mark what is new or moved.

What I found walking the flow

  • Your 1Payment is hidden. After picking a flight, nothing says “pay next”. The only path is Group details → View everyone's flights → Book now. A new user has no reason to open that. Fix: one “Next step” card on Group details whose button changes with your progress and jumps straight to the Book now sheet.
  • NewNo sense of progress. The journey has four steps (Flight, Pay, Preferences, Itinerary) but the app never shows them. The same Next step card carries a 4-segment tracker.
  • Your 2Preferences show too early. The Members page has an “Itinerary preferences” row and Group details has the callout + button even before you have paid. Both appear only when your status is Booked.
  • Your 3Secondary content comes first. Invite link and the friends note sit above the next action. They move below it.
  • Your 4Itinerary has no finish. Only Edit. Add Done.
  • Your 5Redundant hints on the swipe screen.
  • Not changed, for you to decide (a) The Members page and Group details are near copies with different words (Booked vs Ready). Merging them would remove the confusion, but it is a bigger change. (b) After booking, the “Pick flight” row stays; “View your booking” would be clearer. (c) The board button says “Book now” while the sheet says pay; “Review & pay” would match. I left all three alone because you asked to keep the page as is.

1 · Make payment obvious: a “Next step” card

Placed straight under “Your travel group”, where people already look after choosing a flight. One card, one button, and it changes with progress. The tracker is calculated from your status (picked a flight, booked, preferences saved, itinerary built). The flight line comes from your chosen flight; I did not invent a price.

BeforeFlight picked, nothing tells you to pay. The only way in is “View everyone's flights”.
